Introduction




We are second-year middle school students at the Egyptian-Japanese School, Borg El Arab 1, and we are pleased to present our Citizenship Project for the second term. In this project, we showcase what we have learned this semester in the Tokkatsu course, "Building a Citizenship Community."





First, we would like to explain that "Building a Citizenship Community" is a new course that we began studying in the first year of middle school. It aims to teach us how to be good citizens, capable of serving our community and the environment in which we live, and contributing positively to the development of our country. Throughout our studies in this course, we have participated in numerous activities that have helped us understand the meaning of citizenship practically, not just theoretically.








Our project this year focuses on "Heritage and Cultural Sites in Alexandria Governorate." We researched and investigated some of the most important and famous landmarks in our governorate. We worked as a team in class, exchanging information and discussing our findings from various sources. This helped us expand our knowledge and enrich our understanding of our city's history and culture.





It wasn't just about research; some of us also conducted field visits to these heritage and cultural sites, taking authentic photographs that capture their beauty and historical significance. These photos will be featured on our website. This experience allowed us to see the places we had only read about in person, deepening our understanding and connection to them.







After gathering all this information and experience, we discussed as a team the best way to present this content to the community, ensuring we could communicate what we had learned in an organized and effective manner. Ultimately, we agreed to create this website as a platform to compile all the information, photos, and ideas we had gathered. It serves as a simple reference that reflects our collective efforts and highlights the importance of Alexandria's heritage and cultural sites.
